# Critique: src/theme.ts

Method: dual-agent (A: design-review subagent · B: detector subagent)

## Q1: Are the three surfaces distinguishable?
No. Collapse to two surfaces and widen the gaps.
- Lightness gaps (OKLab L) today: background→surface 4.02, surface→raised 3.83, raised→sheet 2.14 (1.06:1).
- Large touching fields need a gap of about 5. The song sheet is already separated by the 0.72 scrim (gap 12.7).
- Proposed:
  - background #0C0E13 (kept)
  - surface #181B21 (gap 5.77)
  - surfaceRaised #262930 (gap 5.92)
  - outline #3E424A
  - delete surfaceSheet
- Checked on #262930: text 12.89, textSecondary 6.09, accent 8.19, danger 4.94.

## Q2: Elevation for the tool row
Dock it with a 1dp rgba(255,255,255,0.08) top edge.
- A dark shadow does not read here: a solid 50% black shadow on background is 1.04:1.
- Overlaying the row would cover the music lane, and RN has no backdrop blur.
- Needs the safe-area bottom inset (edge-to-edge is always on for Android 16).
- Tokens to add: edgeHighlight; layers.toolRow {height 64, borderTopWidth 1, elevation 0}; layers.sheet {top radius 20, top edge, handle 32×4}; shadows.dragClip boxShadow [{offsetY 8, blur 16, rgba(0,0,0,0.6)}]; stroke {hairline 1, selection 2, playhead 2, trimHandle 12}.
